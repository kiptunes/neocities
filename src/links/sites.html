    Webrings

    About

      Webrings are a network of websites grouped by common interest (e.g. liking the same music album, or using the same art program).

      An earlier definition of a webring was commercial and held more stringent rules about the content of its members' websites, but on Neocities nowadays it's more common that the only requirement of a webring is that the overarching theme of the webring is just featured somewhere on your site.

      The members of a webring are linked together so that you can navigate forwards and backwards to the neighbors of the site, and the last site links to the first (in a ring).

    Why is hotlinking bad?

      As defined by Neocities, hotlinking is when a non-HTML file hosted by Neocities is embedded into another site that is not hosted by Neocities, using up the host server's bandwidth.

      Hotlinked files are also susceptible to breaking when the host website is rearranged. It is disallowed for external sites and discouraged between mutually hosted sites.
